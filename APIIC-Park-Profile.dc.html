Does this park have CETP capacity for a 300 kLD load?
Yes, on published figures. The CETP here is operational with 4.2 MLD design capacity and is running at 61% load, which leaves roughly 1.6 MLD — well above a 300 kLD requirement. Final headroom is confirmed at the connection stage.
