STAGE 03 · APPLY FOR LAND

Application, appraisal, allotment

Applications are submitted on the APIIC land application portal, appraised against the Allotment Regulations, and placed before the competent allotment authority. Price is set by the rate order in force on the allotment date.

STAGE 04 · GET APPROVALS

Statutory clearances, run in parallel

Most approvals are not APIIC's to grant — they belong to the pollution control board, the single-window authority, the fire service and the utilities. APIIC's job is sequencing and escalation so they run alongside each other, not one after another.

STAGE 05 · BUILD & COMMISSION

Construction, connections, trial runs

Site handover and boundary demarcation, water and power connections energised, CETP connection commissioned, and access roads confirmed before your first trial run. Construction time is yours; the estate dependencies are ours to keep on schedule.

HOW TO READ THESE TIMELINES

Every duration above is indicative and measured from complete documentation. Stages 3 and 4 overlap in most projects. Nothing on this page creates an entitlement to a timeline; the Citizen Charter carries the committed service levels and the escalation route.
