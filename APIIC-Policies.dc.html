GO MS 12 / 2024IN FORCEVALID 01 APR 2024 – 31 MAR 2029

AP Industrial Development Policy 4.0

The state's umbrella industrial policy. It sets who counts as which enterprise category, what support each category can seek, and how district classification changes the amount.

WHAT IT COVERS

Manufacturing and specified service enterprises setting up or expanding in Andhra Pradesh, across all sectors, from micro units to ultra-mega projects. Sector policies sit on top of it and can add support; they do not replace it.

WHO'S ELIGIBLE
CATEGORY
Micro, Small, Medium
Standard package, higher rebate bands, priority in MSME parks.
CATEGORY
Large
Standard package with employment-linked components.
CATEGORY
Mega & Ultra-Mega
Tailored package, considered by the State Investment Promotion Board.
ADDITIONAL
Recognised entrepreneur categories
Women, SC/ST, BC/minority and differently abled entrepreneurs, as defined in the policy.
WHAT YOU GET
LandAPIIC rate with category and sector rebate bands
Stamp duty & registrationReimbursement on the allotment instrument
PowerCost reimbursement per unit for a defined period
SGSTReimbursement on eligible sales, capped by category
SkillingReimbursement of training cost for local hires
InfrastructureAssistance where trunk works are needed to reach the site
AMOUNTS, CAPS AND CLAIM WINDOWS ARE IN THE OFFICIAL DOCUMENT — THEY ARE NOT SUMMARISED HERE ON PURPOSE
