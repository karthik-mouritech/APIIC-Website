About APIIC

A Government of Andhra Pradesh undertaking that acquires, develops and allots industrial land — and publishes who is accountable for each part of it.

APIIC exists so an investor deals with the owner of the land, not an intermediary. It acquires and banks land, notifies and develops parks, builds the trunk infrastructure, allots plots under published regulations, and stays on afterwards as the estate authority.

What we do

01Acquire and bank land for industrial use, under the state's land acquisition framework.
02Notify and develop parks — roads, drainage, water, power distribution, CETP and STP.
03Allot plots under the Industrial Parks Allotment Regulations at approved rates.
04Run the estates as the authority, including services, maintenance and aftercare.
05Facilitate investors through the approvals landscape, alongside the single-window authority.

What we don't do

Being clear about the boundary saves investors time:

—We do not grant environmental, factory, fire or labour approvals — those authorities do.
—We do not set power tariffs; APERC does, and the discoms supply.
—We do not decide incentive eligibility; the competent authority does, under policy.
—We do not broker private land outside APIIC estates.
