HOW WE MONITOR THESE

Every destination is checked automatically every 15 minutes. Where APIIC owns the destination it is served on standard HTTPS — no non-standard ports, which corporate firewalls block. Where APIIC does not own it, we label it, monitor it, publish the last check time and offer an alternative when it is down.
