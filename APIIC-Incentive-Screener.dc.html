AP Electronics & Semiconductor Policy 2024–29

Industries & Commerce Department · GO ms 31 / 2024
In force Valid to 31 Mar 2029
Why this may apply to you
✓You selected Electronics & Semiconductors, a notified sector under this policy.
✓₹200–1,000 Cr falls inside the Mega category thresholds this policy addresses.
✓Chittoor sits inside a notified electronics cluster, where additional built-space support exists.
What it broadly offers

Capital support on fixed investment, power cost reimbursement for a defined period, SGST reimbursement on eligible sales, built-space rental support inside notified clusters, and reimbursement of skilling costs for local hires. Amounts, caps and claim windows are set out in the policy.

AP Industrial Development Policy 4.0 (2024–29)

Industries & Commerce Department · GO ms 12 / 2024
In force Valid to 31 Mar 2029
Why this may apply to you
✓You identified as a Mega enterprise — the umbrella policy sets tailored packages at this scale.
✓640 direct jobs with 75% local hiring engages the employment-linked provisions.
⚠Mega packages are negotiated case by case and require State Investment Promotion Board consideration.
What it broadly offers

Land at APIIC rates with category rebates, stamp duty and registration reimbursement, power cost support, SGST reimbursement, and infrastructure assistance where trunk works are needed to reach the site.
