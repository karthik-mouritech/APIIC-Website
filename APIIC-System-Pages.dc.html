PRIVACY & COOKIES · PAG-039

What we collect, why, and how long we keep it.

Written for the three places this website actually touches your data: enquiry forms, analytics and cookies.

Enquiry forms

Name, organisation, role, contact details and what you tell us about the project. Used only to respond and to route the enquiry to the right desk. Retained 3 years, then deleted.

Analytics

Aggregate journey measurement, consent-gated. No non-essential tag loads until you consent, and you can withdraw at any time. IP addresses are truncated before storage.

Cookies & local storage

Essential only by default: language choice, alert dismissal and your park shortlist — stored in your browser, never sent to us, never tied to an identity.

YOUR RIGHTS
Ask what we hold about you, and get a copy
Ask us to correct something that is wrong
Ask us to delete an enquiry you no longer want on file
Withdraw analytics consent, without losing access to anything
PROCESSORS

Hosting and analytics are provided by contracted processors bound by data-processing agreements. No investor data is sold or shared for marketing.
