Apply for land allotment

Apply for a plot in any APIIC park, and track the appraisal to the allotment decision.

Who it's for

Any legally constituted entity — company, LLP, partnership, proprietorship or society — proposing a manufacturing or specified service enterprise in Andhra Pradesh. Existing allottees seeking additional land use the expansion service instead.

What you need before you start

✓Incorporation / constitution documents
✓Detailed project report
✓Means of finance / bank appraisal
✓Power and water load estimate
✓Effluent and emission profile
✓Preferred park and plot size

Steps

01Register on the APIIC land application portal and complete the applicant profile.
02Select the park and plot, upload the documents above and pay the processing fee.
03The zonal office scrutinises the application and may seek clarifications once.
04The competent authority decides the allotment under the Allotment Regulations.
05On allotment, pay per the demand notice and execute the agreement; site is then handed over.
Service level & escalation
Decision within 30 days of complete documentation. If that lapses, escalate to the Zonal Manager, then the General Manager (Investor Services), then the grievance route in the Citizen Charter.

Investor FAQs

The questions the facilitation desk answers most. Each one names where the authoritative answer lives.

Does browsing this site or shortlisting a park reserve land?−
No. Nothing on this website reserves, allots or prices land bindingly. The shortlist lives in your browser. Allotment happens only under the APIIC Industrial Parks Allotment Regulations, on application, with a decision by the competent authority.
Authoritative Source: Allotment regulations
Are the rates on park pages the price I will pay?+
They are indicative. The final price is set by the approved rate order in force on the date of allotment, adjusted for any rebate you qualify for, and excludes registration, connection and maintenance charges.
Authoritative Source: Rate order 214/2026
What does "Not published" mean on a park page?+
It means APIIC does not currently publish that field for that park — not that the facility is absent. We show the gap rather than hiding the row, and the zonal desk can usually confirm it in writing on request.
Authoritative Source: Data & freshness dashboard
Can APIIC confirm my incentive eligibility?+
No. The screener is indicative and explains which of your answers made each policy appear. Eligibility is determined by the competent authority under the official policy document, and Mega packages go before the State Investment Promotion Board.
Authoritative Source: Incentive screener
How long does the whole journey take?+
Indicatively: 1–2 weeks to explore, 2–6 weeks to choose a location, 4–10 weeks to allotment, then approvals in parallel over 6–16 weeks. Construction time is yours. Stages 3 and 4 usually overlap.
Authoritative Source: Investor guide
Who do I contact if a portal is down or an officer doesn't respond?+
Portal status and alternatives are published on the official portals page. Unanswered enquiries escalate automatically after two working days to the General Manager (Investor Services); formal complaints go through the grievance route.
Authoritative Source: Help me choose
