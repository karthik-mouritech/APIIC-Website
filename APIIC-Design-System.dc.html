APIIC WEBSITE 5.0 · PROMPT 0 · DESIGN SYSTEM

Cadastre, warmed up

The register logic stays — every park, plot and dataset is a record with an identity, an owner and a verification stamp. What sits on top of it is now a warmer, deeper skin: a royal-blue gradient for hero and headers, cyan carrying the accent and the highlight, six pastel tints reserved strictly for icon chips, and soft 14px cards with a lifted shadow in place of flat hairline boxes. Radius 10–14px, pill buttons, and one glass panel per hero.
