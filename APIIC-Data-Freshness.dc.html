What "verified" means

A named officer in the owning function has checked the value against its source system on the date shown, and a second officer has approved publication. Verification is about the value being current — not about it being favourable.

What happens when a dataset goes overdue

Every figure drawn from it turns amber across the site and reads "review overdue" beside the last verified date. It is never quietly presented as current. Three overdue datasets are visible above; each has an owner and a review date.

Something looks wrong?

Tell us and we will check it against the source. Corrections are logged with the date they were made.
