How apiic land works · four plain steps
01

What APIIC owns

APIIC holds and develops the industrial land itself — it acquires, notifies, lays out infrastructure and allots plots. You deal with the owner, not a broker.

02

How allotment works

You apply on the land application portal. The zonal office appraises the project against the Allotment Regulations, and the competent authority allots. Browsing this site reserves nothing.

03

What determines the price

The approved rate order in force on the allotment date, adjusted by any rebate you qualify for under policy. Rates shown on park pages are indicative and dated.

04

After allotment

Site handover and demarcation, utility connections, construction within the stipulated period, then estate services and aftercare from the estate officer.
